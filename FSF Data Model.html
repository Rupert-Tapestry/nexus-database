17 entities · the commercial spine end to end

The shape of it

A customer buys a package, which raises an invoice, which is settled by a transaction, which activates a purchase. The purchase is the subscription: one live purchase is one Tapestry setting, and everything the integration cares about hangs off that join.

17 entities · the customer record, money in, money out

Customers & billing

A Nexus customer is a forum member with commerce data attached — there is no separate customer ID. Invoices are the billing record of truth; transactions are individual payment attempts against them.
